Database Audit Pricing You will be charged for Database Audit only after it is enabled. For how to enable Database Audit, see Enable the audit service.
Database Audit is charged in pay-as-you-go mode based on the audit log storage size. The billing cycle is one hour. If the duration is less than one hour, the fee will be charged by one hour.
Note:
The audit log storage size is reported at the 'second-level', which may cause precision errors when it is calculated in 'hourly' units. For specifics, the bill shall prevail.

Release Description After audit is enabled for TencentDB for PostgreSQL (pay-as-you-go mode/yearly/monthly subscription mode), when the user releases the cloud database instance or the instance is released upon expiration, the corresponding Database Audit service will be terminated, and audit logs will be automatically deleted and cannot be recovered.
Overdue Payments
1.
When your account balance becomes negative:
You can continue to use your database audit for 2 hours from the moment your account becomes negative. We will also continue to bill you for this period.
When your account has overdue payments for two hours, the database audit service will be automatically stopped, and the billing will stop.
2.
After the service is automatically stopped:
If you top up your account to a positive balance within 24 hours after the database audit is automatically stopped, it will be resumed, and the billing will continue; otherwise, it cannot be resumed.
If your account balance remains negative after 24 hours, the logs will be deleted and cannot be recovered.
